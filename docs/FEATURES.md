# Features — VERSUS

Plain-language list of what the site does, base for Aldo's manual. Full spec: `PRD_VERSUS_v0.6.md`. Maintained by `scribe` at phase end.

## Customer site
- **Compare offers** — the customer chooses luce, gas or luce + gas, home (Casa) or business (Partita IVA), and the type of request (cambio fornitore, subentro, voltura, primo allaccio). How to use it: fill in the blocks on the first page; the site estimates the yearly consumption from the household data, or uses the consumption the customer types in.
- **Offer results** — after a loading screen of about 7.5 seconds (first "Stiamo ricercando per te l'offerta più bassa", then "Ecco le migliori offerte per te!"), the customer sees the offers. The order is: offers highlighted for the customer's audience first, then fixed-price offers from the lowest to the highest estimated monthly cost (VAT excluded), then variable-price offers. Offers of the customer's current supplier are not shown; the monthly consumption can be edited and the costs update instantly. For luce + gas, the luce offer is chosen first, then the gas offer.
- **Highlighted offers ("In evidenza")** — Aldo decides which offers are highlighted for home customers, business customers, or both. If he highlights none for an audience, the cheapest fixed-price offer of that energy type gets the badge automatically.
- **Variable-price offers (luce)** — the price follows the electricity market index (PUN). The card shows the fixed extra cost over PUN (for example "PUN + 0,01 €/kWh") and "Varia con il PUN" instead of a monthly amount, because the monthly cost depends on the market. Variable offers are always listed after the fixed ones and are never highlighted automatically.
- **Offer details** — "Attiva online in 1 minuto" opens a window with the offer's advantages and a button to continue. If the offer requires payment by IBAN, the window says so: "Per attivare questa offerta è obbligatorio il pagamento con IBAN: ti servirà al passo successivo."
- **Online activation** — two short steps: holder data (step A) and supply data (step B: address, POD/PDR, IBAN, identity document and, optionally, the bill). Under the IBAN field a bold line in a green box reads "Sconto del 20% se paghi con IBAN" (20% discount if you pay by IBAN); it applies to every offer. If the chosen offer requires IBAN, the field is mandatory and the site says so. The site checks the codes as they are typed, recognizes the electricity distributor from the POD, and accepts up to 2 document photos or PDFs (photos are reduced automatically; the customer can remove a file).
- **Bill upload (optional)** — the customer can add one digital PDF of a bill (up to 4 MB). The site reads it in the browser, without sending it anywhere for analysis, and proposes the POD, PDR, tax code and current supplier in a window called "Dati letti dalla bolletta: verifica e correggi". The customer checks each value and chooses what to keep; nothing typed by hand is overwritten without asking. If the PDF is a scan or cannot be read, the site asks the customer to type the data in. The bill is kept with the request like the identity document, and can be removed.
- **Thank-you page** — confirms the request and shows the number Aldo's consultant will call from. A "Salva il nostro numero" button saves that number to the phone's contacts as "VERSUS Comparatore".
- **Privacy page** — the privacy notice, linked from the footer as "Privacy e cookie". It lists in full the personal data collected (including the bill, when uploaded) and has a Cookie section: the site uses technical cookies only (a 24-hour cookie that keeps a request in progress, and login cookies for the admin area). Form data typed by the customer is kept in the browser tab only until it is closed. There are no analytics or marketing cookies, so no cookie banner is shown.

## Admin area (Aldo)
- **Login** — email and password, optional two-step code (TOTP).
- **Leads** — list and detail of every request, complete or abandoned, with status (nuovo, contattato, in lavorazione, chiuso) and internal notes. The detail shows the chosen offer, whether the offer requires IBAN, the IBAN, document data, and the identity documents and bill (preview/download). Leads can be deleted one by one or in bulk; their files are deleted too.
- **Suppliers** — create, edit, activate/deactivate and delete suppliers; upload the logo (SVG). Aldo's suppliers now include Sentra Energia, Estra Energia, Enegan and Asti Energy, each with its logo.
- **Offers** — create and edit every field of an offer (prices, fixed fee per month or per year, locked months, promo, advantages, conditions). New fields: "Tipo di prezzo" (fixed or variable with PUN; variable is for luce only, with the spread over PUN in €/kWh), "Pagamento con IBAN obbligatorio", and "In evidenza" (for home customers, business customers, both, or none). Offers can be activated/deactivated and deleted. The list shows badges for variable, IBAN-required and highlighted offers. "Prezzo verificato" must be ticked on every active offer before go-live.
- **Settings** — notification email for new leads, "Invia email di prova", callback number shown on the thank-you page, and the go-live checklist.
- **New-lead email** — Aldo receives an email for each completed request.
