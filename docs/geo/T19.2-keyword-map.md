## 5. What to do first (max 8)

1. **Treat the home as the only commercial target and stop expecting it to outrank suppliers soon.** The head-term SERP is suppliers plus ARERA; the home's job now is brand queries, "comparatore luce e gas", and conversion. Authority (T19.3) is the lever.
2. **Get the 5 indexable URLs indexed and discovered**: confirm in Search Console that the home and the four guides are "indexed", and keep the sitemap `lastModified` honest. (Invisible.)
3. **Propose three visible changes to Giuseppe, each separately**: (a) a text block below the FAQ on the home, about 600-900 words from `fonti-guide.md` and canonical facts; (b) a date line "aggiornato al ..." only if it is true; (c) a footer link group to the four guides, which lets the home pass authority to them. All three need his OK; none is assumed.
4. **Invisible fixes now**: `dateModified` on the home's schema, `datePublished` on the guides, real sitemap `lastModified`, "comparatore" in the home `<title>`/description (metadata, tell Giuseppe first).
5. **Build `/chi-siamo` (E) first among new pages** (all facts are ✅), then **A (come scegliere) and B (fine tutela)** (facts exist). Each needs Giuseppe's OK; none is linked from the visible site.
6. **Mark the guides' open facts**: do not claim the 24-hour luce switch as operating until FG 1.10 is resolved; keep euro amounts out.
7. **Skip**: Catania/Sicilia pages (no local facts), per-supplier pages, "più conveniente" claims, rating/offer schema, anything on the four offers pages.
8. **Define the measurement before moving**: the T19.5 panel should include the 11 queries from T19.1 plus the four PAA questions, checked monthly in Search Console, and the brand phrase "VERSUS comparatore luce e gas".
