### 2a. Google Business Profile: eligibility (verified on Google's Italian guidelines page, 2026-11-07)
Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 (it).
- Eligible: a business with a physical location customers can visit, or one that serves customers at their location.
- Address must be real; remote mailboxes not accepted. Showing the address needs fixed permanent signage; one profile per location.
- Service-area business (no storefront): must show a service area (generally not beyond about two hours' drive), and if it works from a private address such as a home it must hide the address.
- Virtual offices are not eligible; a co-working office only with signage, customers received in business hours and own staff present.
- Sales agents and lead-generation agents for companies are not eligible as individual professionals. KEHILA S.r.l.s. as a company could still qualify only if it meets the location rules above.
- Honest assessment: VERSUS is an online comparator. If Aldo does not receive customers at Via Umberto 326 B and does not visit customers, GBP is **not valid** and must not be forced (suspension risk, and a bad address hurts every other listing). If Aldo does meet customers (for example P.IVA clients) at the office or at their site, a service-area or hybrid profile is plausible. Aldo must state which case is true; do not choose for him.
- Category: pick from Google's dropdown (availability of an exact "energy broker/comparator" category not verified). Candidates: "Consulente energetico", "Società di servizi energetici", "Agenzia di servizi". Choose one primary, no keyword stuffing in the business name: name = "VERSUS" or "VERSUS — comparatore luce e gas" only if that is the real trading name.
- Review policy (third-party summaries, official text not fetched): asking customers is allowed; no incentives, no review gating, no staff quotas.
