## Reading

1. **The on-site half is done.** Every crawler gets the full page, the title and H1 carry the phrase, Google has re-read the home and indexed all 8 URLs within a day of each request. Lab performance is 99-100. Nothing on the site blocks ranking.
2. **The site is 6 days old and nobody links to it or names it.** 0 third-party mentions, 0 backlinks known to Bing, Search Console links still empty. Every domain on pages 1-3 for the phrase is 5 to 17 years old or an institution (C). Billoo, Supermoney, ConfrontaTariffe, Segugio and the Portale Offerte rank without the exact phrase in title or H1; puntienergia and Luce-Gas.it, optimised to the letter with dated price tables, sit lower. Position on this SERP follows authority and age, not on-page work. Agent C's estimate (a judgement, not a measurement): about 20% on-page, 80% authority, age and index state.
3. **Google does not yet know "VERSUS" as our name.** For "versus comparatore luce e gas" it returns the generic comparator page 1, as if the word "versus" were not there. No impression for any "versus" query in Search Console. The home with "VERSUS è un comparatore luce e gas…" was crawled only last night, so this can still move by itself, but a name is learnt from other sites and from people searching it, not from our own pages.
4. **"versus comparatore" alone belongs to versus.com**: position 1 with 5 sitelinks, its app on Google Play, its about page, its YouTube channel (585K), then Instagram, TikTok, Trustpilot, HDblog and TechRadar pages about it. Google also reads "comparatore" as the measuring tool (shopping carousel of dial gauges). Bing puts versus.com on page 1 even for "comparatore luce e gas". Being first for the bare two words means outranking a global brand on its own name: not a realistic target. Realistic brand targets: position 1 for "versus comparatore luce e gas", "versus comparatore luce gas", "versus luce gas", "versus comparatore energia", and the domain query; for bare "versus comparatore", an entry on page 1 below versus.com once Italian users search and click our name.
5. **Bing is the blocker for the assistants.** ChatGPT search and Copilot lean on Bing's index; Bing has known the home since 7 Nov and has not crawled it, with IndexNow accepted twice. Bing usually waits for an outside signal on a brand-new domain. Until Bing, Brave and DuckDuckGo hold a page, the assistants cannot retrieve us (E: 0 of 20 searches). The failure mode stays the safe one ("nothing found about this name", no invented phone), but an unknown name reads as suspicious.
6. **First real traffic will come from the guides.** They already collect impressions at positions 18-50 for allaccio, voltura, subentro and cambio fornitore queries, two days after indexing.
7. Honest forecast: nothing guarantees a position. Positions 1-2 for the phrase are ARERA's portal and ARERA; among private comparators, page 1 is held by Segugio, Altroconsumo, Billoo, Facile.it, VIVI, Supermoney, ConfrontaTariffe. Entering the first 100 needs the first outside links; pages 2-3 are a matter of months of off-site work; page 1 is a long-run goal.
