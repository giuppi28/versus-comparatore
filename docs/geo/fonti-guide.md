# Fact sheet for the guide pages (T17.11)

Prepared 2026-11-05. Prose is English, Italian terms are kept. Every fact has: the statement (paraphrased, no quote over 15 words), the exact source URL, the date shown on the source, and a status.

- VERIFIED = I read it on that primary page in this session (WebFetch of the page itself). Search-engine snippets and summaries never count as verification.
- UNVERIFIED = could not confirm on a primary page. Do not publish.
- "Date: none shown" means the page carries no publication or update date. Almost all ARERA "Atlante per il consumatore" pages are undated, so a stale page cannot be ruled out. Prefer wording that points to ARERA rather than asserting a timing or amount as permanent.
- Access limits in this session: bolletta.arera.it (Bolletta 2.0 glossary) refused the connection; PDF texts (delibera 58/2026 full text, Agenzia delle Entrate circolari) could not be decoded; normattiva.it returned only the navigation tree of DPR 633/72 Tabella A. No comparator or supplier page was used as a source.

## Publishing rules that follow from this sheet

1. Do not publish any euro amount, price or percentage of the bolletta except where this sheet says it is stable. Say "fissato da ARERA e aggiornato periodicamente".
2. Luce switching: say what ARERA's page says (di norma uno-due mesi) and that ARERA approved a faster process in March 2026. Do not claim "24 ore" as already operating (see 1.9, 1.10).
3. Use ARERA's wording for protected services: "Servizio a Tutele Graduali" (STG), "maggior tutela" for vulnerable luce customers, "servizio di tutela della vulnerabilità" for gas.
4. Name PUN as "PUN Index GME" (see 5.12 to 5.14).

## 1. Cambio fornitore

- 1.1 The incoming seller (venditore entrante) starts the switching procedure; the customer signs a new supply contract and the new seller also handles ending the old one. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-mercato-dellenergia/il-mercato-libero/cosa-occorre-fare-per-cambiare-venditore . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.2 Luce: the customer gives the new seller a power of attorney, and the new seller sends the recesso to the old seller on the customer's behalf. The page does not state the notice length. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/recesso/come-avviene-il-recesso-per-cambiare-venditore . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.3 Luce: in general changing seller costs the customer nothing; possible costs are imposta di bollo (only if tax rules require it) and a deposito cauzionale or other guarantee (only if the contract provides). Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-mercato-dellenergia/il-mercato-libero/cambiare-venditore-ha-un-costo . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.4 Luce: fixed-price, fixed-term contracts may carry a fee for early recesso; the maximum must be stated in the contract, specifically signed by the customer, and listed in the Scheda sintetica section "Modalità e oneri per il recesso". Same source as 1.3. Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.5 Gas: recesso has no cost; any cost comes from signing the new contract (bollo, deposito or guarantee). Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/recesso/il-recesso-ha-un-costo . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.6 Gas: switching seller causes no interruption of supply and no technical intervention on the meter or the installations. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-mercato-dellenergia/il-mercato-libero/se-cambio-venditore-puo-interrompersi-fornitura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.7 Luce: switching to the free market is free, needs no meter replacement and causes no supply interruption (ARERA end-of-STG page). Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/clienti-vulnerabili-e-servizio-tutele-graduali-1 . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 1.8 Timing per ARERA's Atlante (gas section): the actual passage normally takes one to two months; switches take effect on the 1st of a month; if the incoming seller starts the procedure by the 10th, the switch is on the 1st of the following month, otherwise one month later. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-mercato-dellenergia/il-mercato-libero/quanto-tempo-serve-per-passare-dal-vecchio-al-nuovo-venditore . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED (page may predate the luce reform in 1.9; use as "di norma")
- 1.9 Delibera 58/2026/R/eel (adopted 3 March 2026, published 6 March 2026) reforms luce switching: technical procedure reduced to 24 hours of one working day for domestic customers who are not morosi, and the whole switch within three weeks at most. Source: https://www.arera.it/atti-e-provvedimenti/dettaglio/26/58-26 . Date: 3 March 2026 (published 6 March 2026). Status: VERIFIED (that it was adopted)

For domestic customers the incoming seller generally waits for the period to end before starting the switch, unless the customer asks to start earlier. Source: same as 1.8. Date: none shown. ## 2. Voltura

- 2.1 Gas definition: voltura is the change of holder of the supply contract, with the same seller and without interruption of gas delivery. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/cose-la-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.2 Luce definition on a luce-specific ARERA page: not read (only the how-to page below). Status: UNVERIFIED
- 2.3 Luce: the new customer applies to the seller already supplying the service, or, if changing seller, to the new seller, using that seller's procedure. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/come-si-richiede-la-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.4 Documents (luce): the customer must show a title proving ownership, lawful possession or lawful detention of the property; a self-declaration is accepted. Same source as 2.3. Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.5 Luce: the seller may refuse a voltura if the title is not shown; in the free market it may refuse for other reasons too; an existing seller must notify a refusal within three working days. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/il-venditore-puo-rifiutare-la-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.6 Timing luce (no seller change): about 5 working days, made of up to 3 working days for seller acceptance plus up to 2 working days for registration of the new contract. With a seller change the page gives no total. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-ottenere-la-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.7 Timing gas: about 4 working days: 2 working days seller acceptance plus 2 working days registration. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-ottenere-la-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.8 Cost luce, free market: depends on the individual contract. For customers in maggior tutela or STG the page says 23 euro to the seller for administrative costs, and no fee owed to the distributor. Source: https://www.arera.it/en/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/quanto-costa-effettuare-una-voltura-o-un-subentro . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED for the free-market "depends on contract" and "no distributor fee"
- 2.9 The 23 euro for tutela/STG customers: ARERA's other page says tutela customers making a voltura did not pay it during 2024 (https://www.arera.it/consumatori/fine-tutela-ele). Two ARERA pages, different periods, current rule unclear. Status: UNVERIFIED (omit the amount)
- 2.10 Cost gas: the distributor charges the seller a contribution listed in the distributor's published prezzario (no amount on the page); what the customer pays depends on the contract; customers in the vulnerability service pay the seller a further 23 euro. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/quanto-costa-effettuare-una-voltura-o-un-subentro . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.11 Other possible costs when signing: imposta di bollo and deposito cauzionale if applicable (see 1.3). Status: VERIFIED
- 2.12 Arrears: in general the seller cannot charge the previous holder's unpaid bills to who takes over by voltura or subentro, except where the new holder is not a stranger to the debt (examples: heir, person living with the previous holder). The seller may ask for a self-declaration. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/il-venditore-puo-addebitare-la-morosita-del-precedente-intestatario-in-caso-di-subentro-o-di-voltura . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 2.13 Difference from subentro: voltura keeps the supply active with the same seller; subentro is a new activation after the previous contract ended (see 3.1). Status: VERIFIED
- 2.14 Voltura together with a seller change: gas rules were completed by delibera 323/2025/R/com (8 July 2025, published 11 July 2025), which lets the customer choose the counterparty during the voltura. Source: https://www.arera.it/atti-e-provvedimenti/dettaglio/25/323-25 . Status: VERIFIED (delibera exists and content); the application date (a search snippet says 1 July 2026) is UNVERIFIED
- 2.15 Luce: single request for voltura plus switching available since 30 September 2021: seen only in a search snippet. Status: UNVERIFIED
- 2.16 Who requests it in practice (outgoing holder vs incoming holder) and whether the outgoing holder must sign: not stated on the pages read. Status: UNVERIFIED

## 3. Subentro

- 3.1 Definition: activation of a supply by a new customer after the previous customer ended the contract (the meter deactivated); unlike voltura it equals a reactivation. Source: https://www.arera.it/en/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/cose-il-subentro . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 3.2 Gas: request to the chosen seller; the seller sends the activation request to the distributor within 2 working days; the distributor activates within 10 working days (counted from receipt of the safety documentation if the plant was modified); automatic compensation on the bill if the distributor is late (amount not on the page). Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/come-si-richiede-il-subentro-e-quanto-tempo-occorre . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 3.3 Luce: same route; seller to distributor within 2 working days; distributor activates within 5 working days; automatic compensation if late. Source: https://www.arera.it/en/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/come-si-richiede-il-subentro-e-quanto-tempo-occorre . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 3.4 Documents: a title proving ownership, lawful possession or lawful detention of the unit; self-declaration accepted (both fuels). If a gas plant was expanded, subject to extraordinary maintenance or converted from another gas, the seller asks for the safety documentation. Sources: 3.2 and 3.3 pages. Status: VERIFIED
- 3.5 Gas costs: the distributor charges the seller 30 euro for meters up to class G6; 47 euro net of taxes for the documentary safety check on a modified plant up to 35 kW (due even if the outcome is negative); 35 euro for each intervention that requires suspension. The seller may pass on to the customer at most what it actually paid. The customer's cost depends on the contract. Sources: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-contratto/voltura-e-subentro/quanto-costa-effettuare-una-voltura-o-un-subentro and https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/la-fornitura/attivazione/quanto-costa-laccertamento-della-sicurezza-dellimpianto . Date: none shown (the acts cited are 737/2022/R/gas, 100/2023/R/com, 40/2014/R/gas). Status: VERIFIED (amounts are set by ARERA acts; recommend not publishing the euro figures)
- 3.6 Luce costs: see 2.8 and 2.9 (the ARERA page covers voltura and subentro together). Status: UNVERIFIED for the 23 euro, VERIFIED for "no fee to the distributor"
- 3.7 Arrears of the previous holder: see 2.12. Status: VERIFIED
- 3.8 Luce: when a customer's own supply is suspended for non-payment, the distributor postpones activation until the debt is paid and tells the seller. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/la-fornitura/attivazione/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-lattivazione . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED as stated on the page (it concerns the requesting customer's own arrears)
- 3.9 What happens when the meter was disconnected for the previous holder's arrears and a different person asks for subentro: no ARERA page I read treats this case explicitly (the gas subentro page says nothing on it). The self-declaration route under DPR 445/2000 appears only in a search snippet. Status: UNVERIFIED
- 3.10 Costs of reactivating a meter disconnected for arrears (distributor price list): not found. Status: UNVERIFIED

## 4. Primo allaccio (nuova attivazione)

- 4.0 Terminology: ARERA's pages use "allacciamento" (the physical connection by the distributor) and "attivazione" (start of supply). "Primo allaccio" is a commercial label not found on the pages read; map it to allacciamento plus attivazione in copy. Status: UNVERIFIED (as an ARERA term)
- 4.1 Luce: for connection only, the customer contacts the local distributor; for connection plus supply, the customer signs with a seller asking for "allacciamento e attivazione" and the seller forwards the request to the distributor within 2 working days. The request states the withdrawal point location, supply voltage and power requested. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/la-fornitura/allacciamento/come-si-richiede-lallacciamento . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 4.2 Luce timing: the distributor gives the preventivo within 15 working days; simple works (meter installation, possibly adjusting the socket) within 10 working days of accepting the quote; complex works up to 50 working days. Seller: 2 working days to forward and 2 to pass the quote on. Times exclude customer works and permits (for example condominium approval). Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/la-fornitura/allacciamento/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-ottenere-lallacciamento . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 4.3 Luce activation when the point is already connected: the distributor activates within 5 working days of receiving the request, which the seller forwards within 2 working days. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/la-fornitura/attivazione/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-lattivazione . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 4.4 Gas timing: simple works: quote within 15 working days, connection within 10 working days of accepting; complex works: quote within 30 working days, connection within 60. The request goes usually through the seller. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/la-fornitura/allacciamento/quanto-tempo-occorre-per-ottenere-lallacciamento . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 4.5 Luce costs, ordinary permanent connection: made of a distance share (straight line to the nearest cabin in service for at least five years) and a power share; homes at the resident address with up to 3.3 kW available pay a fixed share regardless of distance; for supplies up to 30 kW with a power limiter the available power is 10% above the power requested. The page gives no euro amounts; the tables are in the TIC (Allegato C to delibera 616/2023/R/eel) and were not read. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/elettricita/la-fornitura/allacciamento/quanto-costa-un-allacciamento-permanente-ordinario . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED for the structure; euro amounts UNVERIFIED
- 4.6 Gas costs: set in the distributor's preventivo according to the criteria in its prezzario; the page gives no amounts. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/la-fornitura/allacciamento/quanto-costa-lallacciamento . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 4.7 If activation is requested too: costs of signing the contract (bollo, deposito if applicable) and, for gas, the plant safety check at 47 euro net of taxes charged to the seller (see 3.5). Status: VERIFIED
- 4.8 A fixed administrative contribution to the distributor in the luce connection cost and "the distributor cannot charge anything not in the quote": appear only in search snippets. Status: UNVERIFIED
- 4.9 Amount of the automatic compensation for late connection (luce, gas): not found (the pages point to separate pages not read). Status: UNVERIFIED
- 4.10 Luce vs gas in copy: same flow (customer or seller asks, distributor quotes and builds, then seller activates); different working-day limits (4.2 against 4.4); luce quote states cost and maximum time; gas cost follows the distributor's prezzario. Status: VERIFIED (derived only from 4.1-4.6)

## 5. Concetti sulle offerte

### Regimi di mercato (stato a novembre 2026)

- 5.1 Luce, maggior tutela: since 1 July 2024 only vulnerable customers can choose it. Criteria listed: age 75 or over, bonus sociale elettrico, bonus for serious health conditions, disability under Law 104/92, emergency housing after a disaster, supply on a minor non-interconnected island. Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/fine-tutela-ele . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.2 Luce, STG: non-vulnerable customers who had not chosen a free-market contract moved to STG from 1 July 2024 with the seller assigned to their zone. Same source. Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.3 STG ends: from 1 April 2027 it is no longer available (reserved to customers without a seller). Non-vulnerable customers who do not choose are moved without interruption or new contract to a free-market offer of their STG seller: minimum 12 months, no early-termination fee, the offer with the lowest estimated annual spend among the seller's STG-available offers in December 2026; summary sent in December 2026, full documents possibly by 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/clienti-vulnerabili-e-servizio-tutele-graduali-1 . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.4 Vulnerable customers in STG go to the Servizio di Maggior Tutela from 1 April 2027; those not yet identified can declare vulnerability with a form attached to bills from September 2026. Same source as 5.3. Status: VERIFIED. Naming nuance (decree wording "servizio di vulnerabilità" in a snippet): UNVERIFIED, use ARERA wording
- 5.5 STG scope for businesses: microimprese (and other non-domestic customers) with only delivery points up to 15 kW, STG from 1 April 2023; small businesses from 1 July 2024; both lists run to 31 March 2027. Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/il-servizio-a-tutele-graduali . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED. What happens to businesses on 1 April 2027: the page does not say (5.3 describes households). Status: UNVERIFIED
- 5.6 Gas: since January 2024 the tutela service cannot be chosen except by vulnerable customers; non-vulnerable domestic customers moved to the free market and those who did nothing to PLACET offers. Vulnerable criteria: holder aged 75 or over, gas social bonus, disability Law 104/92 art. 3, emergency housing after a disaster. Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/fine-tutela-gas-cosa-fare . Date: none shown (the page also holds an outdated luce deadline; do not reuse it). Status: VERIFIED
- 5.7 Gas vulnerability service price: monthly variable, based on the monthly average of the Italian wholesale price PSV day-ahead, plus other ARERA-set charges and taxes. Same source as 5.6. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.8 Gas raw-material component for that service (CMEM,m) is updated by ARERA as monthly average PSV day-ahead and published within the first two working days of the next month. Source: https://www.arera.it/area-operatori/prezzi-e-tariffe/valore-cmemm-vulnerabili . Date: none shown (latest month listed: October 2026). Status: VERIFIED. Values change every month (October 2026: 88,3585 euro/MWh; September 2026: 78,8021 euro/MWh): recommend omitting
- 5.9 Portale Offerte (ilportaleofferte.it): official ARERA comparison of luce, gas and dual offers, covering PLACET, free-market offers and tutela services, built by Acquirente Unico under the 2017 Legge Concorrenza; ARERA describes it as free and independent. Sources: https://www.ilportaleofferte.it and https://www.arera.it/consumatori/clienti-vulnerabili-e-servizio-tutele-graduali-1 . Date: page shows version V. 2.0.23, no date. Status: VERIFIED

### Prezzo fisso, variabile, PLACET

- 5.10 PLACET (Prezzo Libero A Condizioni Equiparate di Tutela): standard offers every seller must include for households and small businesses (Law 124/2017); in a fixed-price and a variable-price version; indefinite term; conditions renewed every 12 months with at least three months' notice; ARERA fixes the contract terms; luce and gas cannot be bundled in one PLACET contract. Source: https://www.arera.it/consumatori/offerte-standard-per-i-clienti-finali-placet . Date: page last updated 3 November 2026 (as shown); contract templates effective 1 July 2025. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.11 Variable-price offers: price changes automatically at set intervals based on an index; the Scheda sintetica must state the indexing mechanism and the highest price in the previous 12 months with its period. Source: https://www.arera.it/atlante-per-il-consumatore/gas/il-mercato-dellenergia/le-offerte-commerciali/cosa-sono-le-offerte-a-prezzo-variabile . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED. Fixed price: the PLACET page says the price stays fixed for a period of time; "for at least 12 months" appears only in a snippet. Status: UNVERIFIED for the 12-month figure
- 5.12 PUN and PUN Index GME: GME defines the PUN Index GME as the reference price of electricity traded on the day-ahead market (MGP), calculated under art. 13 D.Lgs. 210/21 and the MASE decree of 18 April 2024. Source: https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/it-it/Home/Pubblicazioni/Indici-GME/PUNIndexGme . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.13 Calculation: average of the zonal MGP prices weighted by purchases (GME glossary, https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/Home/Glossario ); ARERA's technical sheet of 28 May 2024 says GME would publish it from 1 January 2025, computed like the old PUN, as part of the move to zonal prices (https://www.arera.it/schede-tecniche/dettaglio/superamento-del-prezzo-unico-nazionale-e-relativa-componente-perequativa-orientamenti-dellautorita ). Status: VERIFIED for definition and method. That it has been in force since 1 January 2025: the ARERA sheet is forward-looking and I found no page stating the start as accomplished fact: UNVERIFIED (say "PUN Index GME, che ha sostituito il PUN" only after confirming; or write "il vecchio PUN, oggi PUN Index GME" with this caveat noted to Giuseppe)
- 5.14 ARERA's own PLACET page (updated 3 Nov 2026) still says "PUN" for the luce variable price, so market wording is mixed. Source: as 5.10. Status: VERIFIED
- 5.15 PSV (Punto di Scambio Virtuale): virtual trading point for exchanges and transfers of gas, run by Snam Rete Gas under AEEG delibera 22/04, per GME's glossary. Source: https://www.mercatoelettrico.org/Home/Glossario . Date: none shown. Status: VERIFIED.
