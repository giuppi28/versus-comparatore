Observations:
1. Both ranking comparators use **Product + AggregateRating + AggregateOffer** to earn the star-and-price snippet. We have no reviews and no verified savings method, so we must not copy this (canonical facts ⚠).
2. Every comparator puts a **month/year in a heading or a card** ("Novembre 2026", "Aggiornata il"). Our home shows no date at all.
3. Depth gap: the home is about 330 words against 2,000-4,400. The guides (900-980 words) are close to the length of typical informational competitors.
4. Authors are teams ("Redazione ..."), not people. A named company behind the site is the norm and we already have it in schema.
5. Selectra and Segugio expose ARERA as the source of price data; ComparaSemplice says it uses Portale Offerte data (from search snippets, not verified on the page).

What makes a source citable (working hypothesis, consistent with the sources above, **not tested on Italian assistants**): it is a primary or regulator source (ARERA); it answers one question per section in the first sentence; it is dated; the organisation behind it is named consistently across the site, schema and third-party listings; its text is in the raw HTML (VERSUS passes: server-rendered). Comparison data is cited only when it is traceable to a visible method.

## 6. Limits of this study
- One google.it snapshot from one machine, one day; positions will move.
- SOStariffe, ComparaSemplice and supplier pages were not analysed in depth.
- AI-assistant citation behaviour is unverified; see §4.
- No traffic, backlink or keyword-volume data (Ahrefs/Similarweb connectors need authorisation and were not used). Difficulty ratings are qualitative.
